About the conference

RIPE 2027 brings researchers and industry together to share ideas on manufacturing, design, mechatronics, automation and inspection.

Keynote lectures by invited speakers, paper presentations and discussion sessions give delegates a chance to compare methods, take on shared problems and learn from each other. This is the third edition, held in the Golden Jubilee year of the Department of Production Technology.
