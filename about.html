About RIPE 2027

A meeting for manufacturing, design, mechatronics, automation and inspection.

The conference

The Department of Production Technology is organising the 3rd International Conference on Recent Innovations in Production Engineering, building on its history of conferences and workshops.

RIPE 2027 will unite researchers to exchange ideas, address challenges, learn new methods and gain insight through keynote lectures by eminent speakers. The themes are sustainable manufacturing, robotics and automation, Industry 5.0, additive manufacturing, advanced manufacturing, composites, smart materials, surface engineering and resource management.

The Department of Production Technology

The department is one of the key academic units of the Madras Institute of Technology (MIT) Campus. It was established in 1977 as the fifth department at MIT and is celebrating 50 years of excellence, its Golden Jubilee (1977 to 2027).

It has grown into a multidisciplinary hub for manufacturing, automation, robotics and mechatronics. Programmes include B.E. Production Engineering, B.E. Robotics and Automation, M.E. Manufacturing Engineering and M.E. Mechatronics, along with part-time B.E. Mechanical Engineering and Ph.D. research. The department began one of India's earliest full-time M.E. Mechatronics programmes in 1999.

It has hosted and contributed to national events such as AIRPAM, ADFORM, RIPE and RETNAM, besides seminars, summer and winter schools, short-term programmes and FDPs. Research is supported by agencies including AICTE, DST-FIST, UGC-DRS/SAP, DRDO (NRB), DST-SERB, CMRG and TANII, and the department is NBA accredited.

Madras Institute of Technology

In 1949, Shri C. Rajam gave the newly independent India the Madras Institute of Technology, so that the country could build the technical base it needed. It offered courses in Aeronautical, Automobile and Electronics Engineering and Instrument Technology for the first time in India, and later added Production Technology, Information Technology, Computer Technology and Rubber and Plastic Technology. MIT merged with Anna University in 1978. Alumni include Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam and ISRO Chairman K. Sivan.

Anna University

Anna University was established on 4 September 1978 as a unitary university, named after the late Dr. C. N. Annadurai, former Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu. It brought together four technical institutions in Chennai: the College of Engineering, Guindy (1794), Alagappa College of Technology (1944), Madras Institute of Technology (1949) and the School of Architecture and Planning (1957).
