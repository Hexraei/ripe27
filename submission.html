Submission guidelines

How abstracts and papers move from submission to publication.

Abstract deadline has passed. The brochure set 15 July 2026 for abstracts. If you still want to submit, write to ripe2027@mitindia.edu before sending anything.

Steps for authors

  1. Submit an abstract. Email it to ripe2027@mitindia.edu by 15 July 2026.
  2. Wait for the decision. Authors are told whether their paper is accepted by 31 August 2026.
  3. Send the camera-ready paper. Due 30 September 2026.
  4. Register and present. At least one author must register. Registration is described on the registration page.
  5. Publication. Papers that are accepted and presented are published in Scopus-indexed proceedings or journals.

Paper template and format

To be announced The brochure does not set a paper template or page limit. The organisers will publish them here.
